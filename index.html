Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas: send us a message. Please include your device model, iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

I lost my progress. Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes the save.

My purchases are missing on a new device. Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Do I need the internet? No. The game plays offline; the internet is only used for Game Center and purchases.

Privacy Policy
